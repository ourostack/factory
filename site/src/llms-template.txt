# The factory

> The factory is the public measurement store for Desk, a plugin that gives coding agents a durable desk of tracks and tasks. It measures agentic work the way Lean measures a factory floor: where each task's time went, which part added value, and which part was waste. It names no person, no machine and no date. This file indexes the data behind every view, for agents.

Site: https://ourostack.github.io/factory/
Source and raw data: https://github.com/ourostack/factory (the `reports` branch holds the built reports)

## How to walk it

The site walks the four steps Lean uses to see waste, in this order. Read the data in the same order.

1. Follow a task (`#/task/<job>`): one real task end to end, and where its time went. `#/` opens on the finished task with the latest finish position (`finish_order`), named or private: the labeled task (`finish_basis: "labels"`) with the largest `finish_order` in `rollups/tasks.json`.
2. Compare tasks (`#/compare`): every task side by side on one scale, as a stack-up of working time by label and waiting by cause, in hours of all elapsed time (`?mode=all`), hours of agent working time only (`?mode=working`) or each task's shares of its own lead time on a 0 to 100% axis (`?mode=share`), with each task's flow efficiency.
3. Rank causes (`#/causes`): causes of waste ranked by the time they cost, in a Pareto chart; each cause has its own page (`#/causes/<key>`, for example `#/causes/waiting:next_prompt`) with its tasks, its stretches and a prompt that starts an A3, or, when a kaizen issue already works on that cause, a link to it and a prompt to check or extend it.
4. Act (`#/act`): the problems taken on (kaizen issues, `data.json` `kaizen_issues`), each with its countermeasure, its cause where the site maps one (`kaizen_issues[].cause`), and its check, and the issues that own the status line's alarms.

Reference pages: `#/why` (why Lean, the eight wastes and a glossary), `#/about` (how the data gets here and the privacy rules), `#/store` (everything else the store measures).

## Reading the numbers

Every number is an object `{ state, value?, reasons }`. `measured` is the whole figure. `partial` is part of it, and `bound` says whether the true figure is higher (`lower`) or lower (`upper`); when the direction is not known, `data.json` says `unknown` and Desk's files under `rollups/` and `map/` say `null`, with `bound_reason` giving why where Desk states it (`reasons.json` has its words). `unavailable` means not measured: its value is absent or null, and it is never zero. `reasons` are codes; `reasons.json` gives the words the page shows for each. {{DIRECTION}} A rollup also carries `n` of `N` and what it counts. Tasks are ordered by `finish_order`, a position, never a date: the order in which each task's first waste labels landed in the store, then open tasks by their first facts file. Tasks whose labels landed in the same commit share a `finish_group` and are ordered within it by lead time, so the longest of them takes the latest position. A task's `name` is the title of its earliest-opened public pull request, or null for a private task; `name_basis: "partial"` (in `data.json`) means some of its pull requests could not be read, so an earlier one may exist. The store adds `name`, `finish_order`, `finish_group` and `finish_basis` to every row of `rollups/tasks.json`.

"Waiting" means idle time only: lead time minus working time, when no agent was working on the task. It is split by what it waited on (`waited_on`): `next_prompt` (the agent had stopped for the operator's next prompt), `other_task` (a session of the task was running, but its agent was working on another task; the other task is never named), `api_retry`, `tool_failure`, `long_tool_call`, `queue_before_start`, `no_session` (no session of the task was running) and `unknown` (no agent was working on the task, and the cause was not recorded). A gap's `waited_on` is its largest cause; where Desk gives `gaps[].idle_by_waited_on_ms`, that is the gap's exact split. The evaluator's labels (value-adding, necessary, each waste) describe working time only.
